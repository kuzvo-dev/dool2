# Getting help

- **Usage questions, ideas**: post in [Discussions](https://github.com/kuzvo-dev/dool2/discussions). You can also see other users' experiences there.
- **Bugs**: use the [bug report form](https://github.com/kuzvo-dev/dool2/issues/new?template=1-bug.yml).
- **Security issues**: don't post them publicly. Use [private reporting](SECURITY.md).
- **News**: get release news at [dool2.com](https://dool2.com).

Dool2 is still in early development, so we don't offer general user support yet. Replies may be slow, and we appreciate your patience.

Issues and Discussions are in English; Korean is welcome too, and maintainers reply in English or Korean.
